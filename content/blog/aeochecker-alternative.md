Choose AEOChecker.ai if you mainly want low-cost, ongoing tracking of how often a brand appears in AI answers. Choose Aeorch if you need to audit client sites, see what to fix first, compare each client against competitors and send white-label PDFs. They answer different questions, and agencies can run both.

Pricing and features checked on 2026-09-19. AEOChecker.ai's site is largely rendered in the browser, so some details below come from search summaries and third-party reviews and are marked as reported. Confirm them on [aeochecker.ai](https://www.aeochecker.ai/) before quoting a client.

## What Does AEOChecker.ai Do Well?

AEOChecker.ai positions itself as a free answer engine optimization and generative engine optimization checker. From its public pages, it offers:

- **One-time reports** that give a first read on a brand's AEO.
- **A daily analytics dashboard** that tracks prompt presence over time, refreshing every 24 hours.
- **A prompt mention rate**, showing where a brand is and is not mentioned in AI answers.
- **Competitor mentions**, showing which rivals AI engines name.
- **Suggested improvements** alongside the tracking data.

Paid plans are reported from about $22.50 a month billed yearly, with a higher Marketer tier that allows several brands and a shared prompt pool. A free trial is also reported. For a solo consultant who wants a cheap way to see whether a brand shows up, that is a strong offer.

## What Does Aeorch Do Differently?

Aeorch audits the site itself rather than sampling AI answers. A scan scores a site on five things: SEO, AEO, GEO, AI Compatibility and Authority. It returns a prioritized list of fixes ranked by impact and effort, and it can generate `llms.txt` and `ai-plugin.json` files for the site.

For agencies, three features matter most:

- **Competitor comparison:** put a client next to competitors and compare all five scores side by side.
- **White-label PDF export:** on the Agency plan, reports carry your name, logo and color, with no Aeorch mark.
- **Multi-site dashboard:** manage up to 15 client sites and 5 team seats on one $79 plan.

New to the idea of AEO scores? See [what an AEO score is](/blog/what-is-aeo-score) and [what a GEO score is](/blog/what-is-geo-score).

## When Should You Choose AEOChecker.ai?

- You want the cheapest possible entry to AI mention tracking.
- The client cares most about whether ChatGPT or Perplexity names them today, not about site fixes.
- You need daily or scheduled tracking and can build your own client reports from the data.
- You are testing AEO with one brand and do not need multi-client management yet.

## When Should You Choose Aeorch?

- You are selling AEO as a service and need to show each client where they stand against competitors.
- You need a prioritized fix list you can act on, not only a visibility score.
- You want a client-ready white-label PDF without building your own template.
- You cover several client sites and want one plan and one dashboard.

## Can You Use Both?

Yes, and for many agencies this is the best setup. Use Aeorch to audit each client and their competitors, fix the highest-impact items, and hand over a branded report. Use a prompt tracker to watch the 10 to 25 prompts that matter and report movement over time. The audit explains why a brand is absent, the tracker proves whether the fixes worked. ## How to Compare Aeorch and AEOChecker.ai on One Client

1. Run AEOChecker.ai's free check on the client to see how AI engines currently treat the brand.
2. Create a free Aeorch account (20 free page credits, 1 credit per page scored) and scan the client's site.
3. Add two competitors to the comparison view and note which of the five categories the client trails in.
4. Take the top three fixes from Aeorch's ranked list and apply them.
5. Re-check both after the fixes and compare.
