## How Did We Evaluate These Tools?

Agencies have different constraints from a single brand, so we scored every tool on five agency-specific questions:

1. **Multi-client scale:** can one plan cover many client sites, or do you pay per domain?
2. **Competitor benchmarking:** can you show a client next to the sites AI engines cite instead of them?
3. **White-label output:** can you send a report that looks like it came from your agency?
4. **Fix guidance:** does the tool tell you what to change, or only report what happened?
5. **Price per client:** what does the plan cost once you divide it across the sites you manage?

We did not rank the tools with a single score. A tracker and an audit tool answer different questions, and a fair comparison says so.

## What Are the Two Kinds of AEO Tool?

Almost every product in this category falls into one of two groups.

- **Monitors (prompt and mention trackers):** they run a set of prompts against ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and similar engines on a schedule, then report whether your brand is mentioned, cited, and how it is described. They answer "are we showing up?"
- **Auditors:** they inspect the site itself for the technical and content signals that make AI citation possible (crawler access, structured data, answer-ready formatting, authority signals) and return a scored list of fixes. They answer "what is stopping us?"

Neither replaces the other. A monitor without an audit tells you the client is missing from answers and leaves you guessing why. An audit without a monitor gives you a fix list but no long-term proof. If you are new to the distinction, start with [what AEO is](/blog/what-is-aeo) and [how AEO differs from SEO](/blog/aeo-vs-seo-differences).

- **Aeorch: best for audit, fixes and client reporting on a budget.** It scores each site on SEO, AEO, GEO, AI Compatibility and Authority, ranks fixes by impact and effort, compares a client against competitors, and exports white-label PDFs. It does not track prompt-level brand mentions, so it pairs well with a tracker.
- **AEOChecker.ai: best for a quick free check and lightweight monitoring.** It positions as a free AEO and GEO checker with brand monitoring across the major AI engines, plus integrations. See our [side-by-side with AEOChecker.ai](/blog/aeochecker-alternative).

- **Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit: best if the client already lives in Semrush.** Per-domain pricing gets expensive across a portfolio.
- **Ahrefs Brand Radar: best if the agency already pays for Ahrefs** and wants brand-mention data in the same place.

## When Should an Agency Use More Than One Tool?

A common stack looks like this:

1. **Audit and fix (Aeorch):** run a scan on every client and their top three competitors, fix the highest-impact items, and send a white-label PDF.
2. **Prompt tracking (a monitor):** track the 10 to 25 prompts that matter most to each client, and report movement monthly.
3. **Manual spot checks:** run your own prompts in ChatGPT and Perplexity each month to sanity-check the tracker.

For agencies that are early in selling AEO, step one alone is often enough to open the conversation, because a competitor comparison makes the gap visible without any ongoing data feed.
