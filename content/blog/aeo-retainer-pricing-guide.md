Most agencies price an AEO retainer as a flat monthly add-on to an existing SEO retainer, not a separate standalone service, since the underlying work (a scan, a fix list, a report) shares the same client relationship and data. Pricing anchors to the deliverable's effort, not an arbitrary premium for being new.

## Add-On Versus Standalone

A brand-new "AEO service" line item asks a client to evaluate and budget for something they don't have a category for yet. An "AI visibility add-on" to the SEO retainer they already pay for asks them to expand something they've already said yes to. The second framing closes faster, especially with clients who are AEO-curious but not yet AEO-convinced.

## What Actually Goes Into the Retainer

- **A monthly re-scan and report**: the recurring deliverable that justifies a recurring fee.
- **A prioritized fix list**: three to five items per month, since that's what turns a report into billable implementation work.
- **The fixes themselves**, if the agency implements rather than just reports: schema markup, robots.txt corrections, content restructuring.

## Answer Box: How Should the Add-On Be Priced?

As a flat monthly line item sized to the incremental effort of the re-scan, report, and fix list, not as a percentage markup on the base retainer. [SEO retainer pricing benchmarks](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/) commonly land in the low-to-mid hundreds per month for a focused scope; an AEO add-on covering one recurring scan and report sits comfortably in that same range rather than requiring a separate pricing conversation.

## The Trade-Off Worth Naming to the Client

An AEO retainer that only reports scores without implementing fixes is cheaper to run but slower to show results, since the client still has to execute the fixes themselves or route them to a developer. A retainer that includes implementation costs more but compounds faster, since each month's fixes stack on the last. Naming this trade-off upfront, rather than letting the client assume "reporting" means "fixing," avoids a renewal conversation built on a misunderstanding.

## Key Takeaways

- Bundle as an add-on to existing SEO retainers rather than launching a new standalone service
- Price to the deliverable's effort: a scan, a report, a fix list, not an arbitrary AI premium
- Be explicit about whether the retainer reports issues or fixes them, since that changes both the price and the client's expectations
