## SEO, AEO, and GEO: Why You Need All Three

If you're still only thinking about traditional SEO, you're missing two growing channels that are already redirecting significant amounts of search traffic. In 2025, a complete website optimisation strategy covers:

- **SEO** — Search Engine Optimization (Google, Bing)
- **AEO** — Answer Engine Optimization (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews)
- **GEO** — Generative Engine Optimization (LLMs, AI content generators)

Google now serves AI Overviews in the majority of US searches. Perplexity AI reached over 100 million queries per day by late 2024. A 2024 study from Princeton, Georgia Tech, and The Allen Institute ([Aggarwal et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735)) found that specific optimisation techniques — including adding statistics, citing sources, and improving fluency — increased AI citation rates by up to 40%.

This guide explains each one and how to measure and improve all three simultaneously.

## What is SEO?

**Search Engine Optimization (SEO)** is the practice of improving your website so it ranks higher in search engine results pages (SERPs). It's been the core of digital marketing for over 25 years.

Key SEO signals in 2025:
- **Technical**: page speed, mobile-friendliness, Core Web Vitals
- **On-page**: title tags, meta descriptions, H1 structure, canonical URLs
- **Content**: keyword relevance, content depth, internal linking
- **Off-page**: backlinks, domain authority, brand mentions

A strong SEO foundation is still essential — it's table stakes for everything else.

## What is AEO?

**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** is the practice of structuring your content so AI-powered answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews — can extract and present your content as a direct answer.

Key AEO signals:
- **FAQPage schema** — marks up Q&A content with structured data
- **Question-based headings** — H2/H3 tags that contain questions AI engines receive
- **HowTo and Article schema** — structured markup for instructional and editorial content
- **Direct answers** — concise, factual sentences placed immediately after question headings
- **AI bot access** — ensuring GPTBot, ClaudeBot and other AI crawlers can access your site

AEO is particularly important for informational queries — "how to", "what is", "why does" questions that now trigger AI Overviews in Google and direct answers in Perplexity.

## What is GEO?

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the practice of creating content that large language models (LLMs) are confident enough in to generate, cite, or reference.

Where AEO is about *being extracted* from your page, GEO is about *being trusted* as a source. LLMs are cautious about citing pages that look thin, unverifiable, or lacking authority signals.

Key GEO signals:
- **Content depth** — pages should have substantial, original content (300+ words minimum)
- **Author attribution** — who wrote this? Is there a named expert behind it?
- **Publication dates** — when was this written? Is it up to date?
- **External citations** — does your content reference credible external sources?
- **Definitional content** — clear "What is X" and "How to Y" sections signal factual reliability
- **Statistical references** — specific numbers and data points increase credibility

## How AEO and GEO Differ

| | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Being cited in AI answers | Being trusted by AI models |
| Key signal | Schema markup, Q&A structure | Content depth, authorship, citations |
| Tools | FAQPage, HowTo, Article JSON-LD | Author bio, dates, external links |
| Outcome | Quoted in Perplexity/ChatGPT | Referenced in AI-generated content |

In practice, AEO and GEO work together — structured, authoritative content excels at both.

## The Three-Layer Strategy

Think of your optimization as three stacked layers:

**Layer 1 — SEO Foundation**
Get the technical and on-page basics right. Title tags, meta descriptions, H1 structure, canonical URLs, page speed. Without this layer, nothing else works.

**Layer 2 — AEO Superstructure**
Add schema markup and Q&A structure on top of your content. FAQPage JSON-LD, question headings, HowTo markup for tutorials. This layer targets AI answer engines.

**Layer 3 — GEO Authority**
Add authorship, dates, citations, and content depth. This layer targets LLMs that need to trust your content before generating around it.

## Common Mistakes Across All Three

**SEO mistakes:**
- Missing or duplicate title tags
- No meta description
- Blocking crawlers in robots.txt (accidentally)

**AEO mistakes:**
- No FAQ schema even on pages that clearly contain Q&A content
- Question headings that don't start with "What", "How", "Why", "When"
- Blocking GPTBot, ClaudeBot in robots.txt

**GEO mistakes:**
- Articles published without an author name
- Content under 300 words
- No external citations to authoritative sources
- Pages that have no publication or "last updated" date
