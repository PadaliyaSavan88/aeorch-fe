A white-label AEO report needs to remove every trace of the underlying tool, not just add a logo on top of it: no vendor watermark, no footer link, and branding (logo, color, agency name) applied throughout, not just on the cover page. Most reporting tools only do the second part.

## Why "Add Your Logo" Isn't White-Label

Plenty of SEO tools let you upload a logo to a report header. Fewer remove their own mark from the footer, the URL bar if it's a hosted link, or the color scheme underneath. A client who receives a PDF with your logo on page one and a competitor's tool name on page three has learned something about your stack that you didn't intend to tell them. True white-label means the client cannot identify the underlying vendor from the artifact itself.

## What Agencies Actually Need in the Report

- **Full branding, not a logo slot**: agency name, brand color, and logo applied to every section, not a cosmetic header.
- **A score a non-technical client understands**: one overall number, with SEO, AEO, GEO, Authority, and AI Compatibility as the supporting breakdown, not a wall of forty issues.
- **A prioritized fix list**: three to five items ranked by impact, since a client reviewing a report in five minutes needs the headline, not the appendix.

## Answer Box: Why Does White-Labeling Matter for Retention?

A report that looks like it came from the agency reinforces that the agency is the one doing the work. A report that visibly comes from a third-party tool invites the client to wonder whether they could just buy the tool themselves and skip the agency's markup. The visual signal matters as much as the content.

## The Trade-Off: Building This In-House

Building a white-label PDF pipeline in-house means owning PDF generation, brand-token storage, and a template that survives a redesign without breaking. For most agencies that's not the differentiated part of the business, it's overhead standing between the scan and the client meeting. That's the trade-off a scan tool with white-label export built in is solving for: the scoring and the branding both come from one pipeline, so a plan upgrade turns it on rather than a build project turning it on.

## Key Takeaways

- White-label means no trace of the vendor, not just a logo added on top
- Report one overall score with a five-dimension breakdown, not a raw issue count
- Full branding throughout the document is what actually protects the agency relationship, not the cover page alone
